BEHIND THE PROJECT

Curiosity,
taking shape.

An independent microdrone project by Adil.

Combining a passion for drones with hands-on work in embedded systems, sensors and hardware development.

Where the project stands

Concept development and component evaluation. The aircraft images are visual references, not photographs of a completed prototype. The final airframe may differ.

About the locations

The images evoke Delhi, mountain valleys, tea-growing landscapes and river corridors. Exact locations are not verified. They illustrate ideas, not completed deployments.

What comes next

Finalise the mass budget, test propulsion efficiency, validate control electronics and document controlled prototype trials.
